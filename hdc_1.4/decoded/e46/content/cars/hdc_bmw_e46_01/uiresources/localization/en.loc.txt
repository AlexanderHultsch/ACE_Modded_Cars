Carbon roof, a rev-hungry straight-six and plenty of attitude: the M3 CSL brings BMW's driver-focused spirit to Hina's Drift Club. Its naturally aspirated engine answers your right foot directly, while the six-speed SMG and its paddles keep the next gear close at hand.

A complete drift setup and dedicated Hina's Drifters E46 tyres give this coupe its club credentials. Strong throttle response, a playful rear end and a longer, weightier rhythm make it the resident hooligan. It rewards confident inputs and smooth catches, with familiar HDC balance to help you find your feet. Climb in, pick a gear and mind your manners. The BMW probably won't.
